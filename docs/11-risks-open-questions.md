# Risks and Open Protocol Questions

Status: unresolved V1 multi-asset protocol and qualification questions. This
register records open work under [the decisions](00-decisions.md) and
[multi-asset design](v1-multi-asset.md). Experimental behavior, caller-supplied
policies and planning allowances do not implicitly adopt consensus rules.
The retained seven-node network, its history, keys and verifier are unchanged.

| Topic | Required decision or evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering-network-2 activation | Complete the combined shared transition, canonical state import and durable automatic-sync runtime; qualify the changed confidential-fee relation, including recipient recovery and bounded encryption-correctness verification. Preserve existing-network behavior until separate genesis activation. |
| Engineering-network-2 governance | Verify founder-only destination proposals, revocation-only address cancellation, dual-key rotation and signer replacement, and dual-key cancellation of those joint changes before activation. Reject revocation-only joint cancellation, activation-boundary cancellation and sequence replay. |
| Engineering-network-2 recovery | Measure ordinary eight-core catchup and a real node offline for strictly more than 86,400 seconds on the owner-controlled private network. Unit tests and existing-network recovery results cannot substitute for this evidence. |
| Development-fund activation | Supply 1–32 immutable genesis ML-DSA-65 keys, positive basis-point shares totaling 10,000 and the explicit team residue index. The versioned native registry and independent wallet allowances support this format, but launch values remain unset and production fails closed. Each credit releases half after 24 blocks and half over 1,461 height-based days. No V1 key/share replacement is provided. |
| Economic security review | The selected [monetary rules](v1-economics.md), one/five-note settlement, snapshot-height locks and pool/burn/fund rollback have tests and revised source-bound proof profiles. Complete independent review of the combined consensus, AIR, hiding and application-binding model; engineering qualification is not external approval. |
| Public supply metrics | Publish scheduled subsidy locks/circulation and pool/fund counters. Exact private unspent locked holdings cannot be derived without a separate privacy-affecting protocol; do not expose transaction classification or fee openings. |
| Authenticated wallet tree snapshots | Before mainnet, specify and independently review a cumulative commitment-tree root in the block header or its authenticated data, including count/order binding, snapshot verification and reorganization semantics. Current headers do not directly commit that root. Engineering wallets must authenticate historical public commitments to construct the tree; birth-height scanning alone cannot authenticate a supplied tree snapshot. This is a prospective consensus-format change, not an engineering-network activation. |
| Wallet authorization evolution | Preserve the opaque owner commitment width. V2 may add a versioned in-circuit policy and uniform signature slots; V1 does not reinterpret existing owner preimages or reserved-zero ciphertext bytes. See [the V2 design](v2/multisignature.md). |
| Testnet issuance-fee activation | The testnet fee is fixed at 50 SIP (5,000,000,000 atoms), paid to a dedicated development-fund owner. The native transition binds a verified canonical or same-block payment output to the declaration, consumes the receipt once and restores registry state through replay/reorganization. The destination and explicit metadata/per-block limits are not configured: keep issuance admission closed and fail closed until they are authenticated and selected. Qualify the complete wallet/payment path and resource limits before activation. The mainnet issuance fee remains undecided. |
| Non-SIP minimum output | Review the experimental minimum of one atomic unit for non-SIP outputs and 10,000 atoms for SIP. Define the adopted asset-aware rule and its wallet implications before activation; the current engineering profile retains that minimum-output policy pending protocol review. |
| Issuance resource bounds | Adopt canonical metadata/name limits, initial-note limits, record/ciphertext/proof limits and per-block issuance byte and CPU limits. Until then, planning issuance allowances cannot establish complete worst-case bandwidth or permanent storage. |
| Final envelope | Freeze the canonical three-output envelope, version/suite framing and admission identity. The body codec implements 2,027 bytes; the 2,115-byte permanent-position ledger and 2,184-byte envelope prefix include proposed framing and are not activated node formats. |
| Standalone verifier limits | Qualify malformed-proof decoding and allocation, per-proof CPU and charged-memory bounds, concurrency/in-flight limits and full-block verification under the new relation. Prior two-output verifier budgets do not automatically qualify multi-asset admission. |
| Checkpoint delivery for expiring services | Local role retention, compact public records and same-node sealed replay bases do not implement remote checkpoint trust. The engineering transition implements a separate release-pinned ML-DSA-65 2-of-2 roster; it does not activate genesis-reserved authorities or define mainnet trust. Qualify pinned authorities, six-hour authenticated publication, complete permanent-data delivery, proof-tail availability, atomic installation, conflicting-ancestry rejection and long-offline recovery before enabling non-fixture bootstrap/validator expiry. Measure actual fresh-miner download/disk costs and a recovery beyond the retention window. |
| Database and storage overhead | The local 200 GB file-content budget raises an alarm only; validation continues and retention is never shortened. Physical disk exhaustion can still cause I/O failure. Qualify capacity alerts, service availability and post-pruning workloads. Measure actual block/issuance encodings, indexes, state trees, write amplification, WAL and compaction peaks under sustained load. Disk-lifetime estimates remain conditional until these costs and issuance bounds are qualified. |
| Multi-asset admission integration | The revised b7/q41/g20 profile meets the three-trial two-second generation criterion with a 655,360-byte transaction cap. Qualify the complete canonical wallet/envelope/node path under sustained load; the retained two-output decoder remains limited to 524,288 bytes. The cap is not a mathematical bound on every randomized proof. |
| Runtime and optimization evidence | Three qualified proving trials do not bound grinding tails, shared-host contention or whole-node sustained load. Column/opening-size optimization remains separate; adopt smaller caps or lower bandwidth only after a source-bound profile revision and equivalent security/resource measurements. |

Node integration must resolve these questions through one deterministic,
integer-only, atomic block transition shared by production and validation.
Independent security review, wallet recovery, backup restoration, real
24-hour service retention and full-network resource acceptance remain additional
activation gates. No active-network change follows from this document.
