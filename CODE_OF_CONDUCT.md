# Code of conduct

Discuss changes, evidence, and design tradeoffs respectfully. Keep issues,
pull requests, and other project spaces focused on technical work.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, and sharing another
person's private information without consent are unacceptable.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive material. Keep sensitive evidence
out of public discussion. Report technical vulnerabilities through the
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to protect
contributors and preserve productive discussion.
